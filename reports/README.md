# Weekly internship reports

This folder contains the written reports for the six-week YuvaIntern Beauty & Wellness data science project.

- Week 1 — Industry orientation and problem definition
- Week 2 — Detailed report (DOCX)
- Week 3 — Data cleaning and preprocessing pipeline
- Week 4 — Exploratory data analysis and feature engineering
- Week 5 — Predictive modeling and algorithm selection
- Week 6 — Model evaluation, reporting, and potential business impact

The Markdown reports document the methodology and planned work. They do not claim that data analysis, model training, evaluation, or business impact measurements have been completed unless results are explicitly added from actual execution.
